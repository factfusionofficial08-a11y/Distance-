As you read DISTANCE, you'll encounter fragments of its world, conspiracies, theories, hidden connections, and clues scattered throughout the story.
Some cards reveal secrets that belong to the moment you're reading.

Others point toward what hasn't happened yet.
Don't open every card at once.

Some truths are meant to be discovered. Others are meant to remain hidden until the story catches up with them.

Read the book first. Follow the clues. When a card becomes available, decide whether you're ready to uncover what's behind it.

Because in DISTANCE, knowing the truth too early can change the way you see everything.
